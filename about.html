About Intelliseq

Built by scientists,
for the labs doing
real genomics work

Founded in 2014 in Kraków, Poland, Intelliseq has spent a decade building the computational infrastructure that turns raw sequencing data into clinical-grade insights — without the complexity that slows genomics teams down.

Our mission

Making genomic interpretation
accessible, scalable,
and clinically actionable

We believe the bottleneck in precision medicine isn't sequencing — it's interpretation. Intelliseq exists to remove that bottleneck with automation, modularity, and clinical rigor built into every layer of the platform.
