Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data Controller

Intelliseq sp. z o.o., ul. Bobrzyńskiego 14, 30-348 Kraków, Poland is the data controller responsible for your personal data when you use our website and platform.

2. What data we collect

We collect contact information (name, email, organization) when you submit a demo request or contact form. We collect usage analytics to improve our platform. Genomic data processed through iFlow is handled under separate data processing agreements compliant with HIPAA and GDPR.

3. How we use your data

Contact information is used to respond to inquiries and schedule demos. We do not sell personal data to third parties. Genomic data is processed solely for the purposes specified in the applicable data processing agreement.

4. Your rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@intelliseq.com.

5. Security

Intelliseq maintains ISO 13485 certification and implements technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk level of data processing, including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and audit logging.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@intelliseq.com
