What it does

Automated rare disease
reporting that keeps up
with your lab

From WES/WGS or targeted panel data to a complete clinical report — with phenotype integration, ACMG variant classification, and inheritance analysis built in.

Phenotype-driven variant prioritization
HPO-term matching ranks variants by clinical relevance to the patient's specific phenotype, dramatically reducing manual review burden.
ACMG/AMP variant classification
Automated classification following ACMG/AMP 2015 guidelines with evidence coding, supporting transparent and auditable clinical reporting.
Family trio & segregation analysis
Supports proband-only, duo, and trio analysis with de novo calling and inheritance pattern determination for comprehensive rare disease workups.
Clinical-grade PDF reports
Branded, structured reports delivered directly to clinicians — formatted for clinical use, not just bioinformatics review.
